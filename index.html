About Me

Keeping streams

I'm a Broadcast & Linux Engineer based in Chennai, working at the intersection of live TV operations and server infrastructure. Over the years I've built and maintained Ubuntu-based streaming pipelines handling live sports — La Liga, NBA, NFL, Wimbledon — managing everything from satellite downlink and playout scheduling to FFmpeg encoding and SRT/RTMP delivery.

I'm comfortable going deep on both the broadcast side and the Linux server side: Netplan configs, UFW hardening, systemd services, the works. I've deployed and managed cloud infrastructure on DigitalOcean, OVH, and Proxmox, and I've run 24×7 production environments where stream quality isn't optional.

Currently focused on roles combining Linux/cloud administration with broadcast or OTT operations — where uptime matters and there's real engineering depth involved.
